# UPI Payment Request Fraud Advisory

## Overview
UPI Payment Request Fraud, often known as the "Reverse Request Scam", is a widespread financial threat in digital payment systems. Scammers manipulate the UPI architecture by sending a "collect request" or "debit request" to a victim's VPA (Virtual Payment Address). They typically contact the victim claiming a refund, a cashback bonus, or an accidental transfer. The user is instructed to enter their UPI PIN under the false pretense that they are "receiving" money. However, entering a UPI PIN strictly authorizes a debit (sending money), resulting in immediate financial loss.

## Warning Signs
* Receiving a notification asking you to enter your UPI PIN to claim a refund, prize, or cashback.
* The VPA handle of the requester contains suspicious words like "refund", "claim", "mule", or unusual numeric combinations.
* The scammer creates an artificial sense of urgency, insisting the payment will fail if not verified immediately.
* QR codes sent via WhatsApp that route directly to a high-value debit request on scanning.

## Safe Practices
* Entering a UPI PIN is strictly for SENDING money. You never need a PIN to RECEIVE money.
* Do not scan QR codes received from unknown individuals on messaging apps.
* If someone claims they sent money by mistake, instruct them to contact their bank's dispute resolution cell.
* Decline and block any unverified "collect requests" in your UPI app immediately.
